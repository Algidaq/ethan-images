III-24

Founder's letter (signed, long-form)

Before · table

A note from the team

Why we built this.

For a long time, the most expensive credit card in your wallet has been the worst designed. Heavy on plastic, light on humans, generous with fees that nobody chose to pay.

We started Reserve four years ago with a single rule: nothing in the box that you'd want to throw away. The rule shaped the metal. The rule killed the FX fee. The rule stopped us building a points system you'd need a degree to use.

It also slowed us down. The first version was three years late. We're not sorry about that.

— Hassan Mahmoud

Head of Cards · ENBD

Will my old miles transfer over?

Yes. Every Skywards mile in your existing balance ports across automatically when your Reserve card activates. Nothing is lost.

Is the AED 4,200 fee worth it for me?

If you spend more than AED 6,000 a month on travel and dining, the math works without help. Below that, look hard at Signature instead.

What happens if I damage the card?

First replacement is free. Second is at cost. The titanium is a quiet brag — we'd like it to come home in one piece.
